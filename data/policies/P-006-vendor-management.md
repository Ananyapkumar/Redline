## 1. Scope

### 1.1 Covered relationships
This Policy applies to any third party that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI on behalf of Kestrel, or that has access to Kestrel production systems.

## 2. Contracting

### 2.1 Business associate agreement required
A written business associate agreement shall be executed before any PHI is disclosed to a third party.

### 2.2 Subcontractor flow-down
Business associate agreements shall require the associate to obtain equivalent written assurances from any subcontractor that handles PHI.

### 2.3 Breach notification terms
Agreements shall require the associate to notify Kestrel of any suspected breach without unreasonable delay and in no case later than ten (10) calendar days after discovery.

## 3. Due Diligence

### 3.1 Pre-contract assessment
A security assessment shall be completed before contract execution for any vendor that will handle PHI.

### 3.2 Ongoing assessment
Vendors handling PHI shall be reassessed at least every twenty-four (24) months.

### 3.3 Evidence of controls
Kestrel shall obtain and review a current SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent independent assessment where available.

## 4. Termination

### 4.1 Return or destruction
On termination, the associate shall return or destroy all PHI received from Kestrel, and shall certify the same in writing.

### 4.2 Retained data
Where return or destruction is infeasible, the associate shall extend the protections of the agreement to the retained information for as long as it is retained.
