## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Purpose
This Policy governs the development, classification and maintenance of software functions that inform clinical decisions.

## 2. Classification

### 2.1 Device determination
Each clinical software function shall be assessed to determine whether it meets the definition of a device, and the determination shall be documented and approved by Regulatory Affairs.

### 2.2 Criteria for exclusion
A function may be treated as excluded from device regulation only where the basis for that conclusion is documented against the applicable statutory criteria.

### 2.3 Re-assessment on change
A device determination shall be re-performed whenever a change is made that could significantly affect the safety or effectiveness of the software function.

## 3. Transparency

### 3.1 Basis of recommendations
Where a software function provides a recommendation to a health care professional, the system shall make available the basis for that recommendation, including the inputs relied upon.

### 3.2 Independent review
The system shall enable the health care professional to independently review the basis for the recommendation rather than relying primarily on it.

## 4. Change Control

### 4.1 Documented changes
All changes to clinical software functions shall be documented, including the rationale and the assessment of impact on safety or effectiveness.

### 4.2 Validation
Changes shall be validated prior to release in accordance with the Change Management and Release Control Policy.

## 5. Post-Market

### 5.1 Complaint handling
Complaints relating to clinical software function performance shall be recorded and assessed for reportability within five (5) business days.

### 5.2 Performance monitoring
Clinical software function performance shall be monitored on an ongoing basis and reviewed by Regulatory Affairs at least annually.
