## 1. Definitions

### 1.1 Security incident
A security incident is any attempted or successful unauthorised access, use, disclosure, modification or destruction of information, or interference with system operations.

### 1.2 Breach
A breach is an acquisition, access, use or disclosure of PHI not permitted by law which compromises the security or privacy of that information.

## 2. Detection and Reporting

### 2.1 Internal reporting
Workforce members shall report a suspected incident to Security Operations immediately upon becoming aware of it, and in no case later than the end of the same business day.

### 2.2 Triage
Security Operations shall triage and assign a severity level to each reported incident within four (4) hours of receipt.

## 3. Response

### 3.1 Containment
Confirmed incidents shall be contained as soon as practicable, with priority given to preventing further unauthorised access.

### 3.2 Evidence preservation
System logs, images and other evidence relevant to an incident shall be preserved for the duration of the investigation and for six (6) years thereafter.

### 3.3 Risk assessment
A documented four-factor risk assessment shall be completed for each incident involving PHI to determine whether it constitutes a breach.

## 4. Notification

### 4.1 Notification to individuals
Where a breach is confirmed, affected individuals shall be notified without unreasonable delay and in no case later than sixty (60) calendar days after discovery.

### 4.2 Notification to the Secretary
Breaches affecting five hundred (500) or more individuals shall be reported to the Secretary of Health and Human Services contemporaneously with individual notice.

### 4.3 Smaller breaches
Breaches affecting fewer than five hundred (500) individuals shall be logged and reported to the Secretary annually, no later than sixty (60) days after the end of the calendar year.

### 4.4 Media notice
Breaches affecting more than five hundred (500) residents of a State shall additionally be notified to prominent media outlets serving that State.

## 5. Post-Incident

### 5.1 Lessons learned
A post-incident review shall be completed within fifteen (15) business days of closure for any incident rated high or critical.
