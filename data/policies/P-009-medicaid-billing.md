## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Purpose
This Policy establishes controls over claims submitted to State Medicaid agencies and to the Children's Health Insurance Program.

## 2. Claim Accuracy

### 2.1 Documentation before submission
No claim shall be submitted unless the underlying service is documented in the clinical record at the time of submission.

### 2.2 Coding review
Claims exceeding a threshold set annually by Revenue Integrity shall be reviewed by a certified coder before submission.

### 2.3 Prohibited practices
Upcoding, unbundling and the submission of claims for services not rendered are prohibited without exception.

## 3. Coverage Determinations

### 3.1 State plan alignment
Claims shall be submitted only for services covered under the applicable State plan in effect on the date of service.

### 3.2 Excluded services
Revenue Integrity shall maintain a register of services excluded from Federal financial participation, and shall update it within thirty (30) days of a change in Federal or State coverage rules.

### 3.3 Prior authorization
Where a State plan requires prior authorization, evidence of authorization shall be retained with the claim record.

## 4. Overpayments

### 4.1 Identification and return
Identified overpayments shall be reported and returned within sixty (60) days of identification.

### 4.2 Self-audit
Revenue Integrity shall conduct a documented sample audit of submitted claims not less than quarterly.

## 5. Record Keeping

### 5.1 Supporting documentation
Documentation supporting each claim shall be retained in accordance with the Data Retention and Disposal Policy.

### 5.2 Cooperation with audits
Kestrel shall cooperate with State and Federal audit requests and shall produce requested records within the timeframe specified in the request.
