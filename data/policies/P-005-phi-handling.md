## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Purpose
This Policy governs the use and disclosure of protected health information ("PHI") by Kestrel workforce members and systems.

### 1.2 Minimum necessary
Uses and disclosures of PHI shall be limited to the minimum necessary to accomplish the intended purpose, except for disclosures to or requests by a health care provider for treatment.

## 2. Permitted Uses

### 2.1 Treatment, payment and operations
PHI may be used or disclosed for treatment, payment and health care operations without individual authorization, subject to Section 1.2.

### 2.2 Research
PHI may be used for research only with individual authorization, a waiver granted by an Institutional Review Board, or under an approved limited data set arrangement.

### 2.3 De-identification
Data shall be treated as PHI unless de-identified under the expert determination or safe harbour method, and the basis for de-identification shall be documented.

## 3. Access by Workforce

### 3.1 Role limitation
Workforce members shall access PHI only for records within the scope of their assigned duties.

### 3.2 Access to own records
Workforce members shall not access their own patient records or those of family members through internal systems.

## 4. Disclosures

### 4.1 Accounting of disclosures
Kestrel shall maintain an accounting of disclosures of PHI that are not for treatment, payment or operations, for six (6) years preceding the date of request.

### 4.2 Individual right of access
Kestrel shall provide an individual with access to their PHI within thirty (30) days of a request.

### 4.3 Disclosure log
Each disclosure requiring accounting shall be recorded with the date, recipient, purpose and description of the information disclosed.

## 5. Transmission

### 5.1 External transmission
PHI shall not be transmitted to an external party by unencrypted email, consumer messaging application, or personal cloud storage.

### 5.2 Approved channels
Transmission of PHI shall use channels approved by the Privacy Officer and listed in the Approved Systems Register.
