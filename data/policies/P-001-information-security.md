## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This Policy establishes the minimum information security requirements for all systems operated by Kestrel Health Technologies, Inc. ("Kestrel") that create, receive, maintain or transmit company or patient data.

### 1.2 Applicability
This Policy applies to all workforce members, contractors and third parties with access to Kestrel systems, without exception for pilot, development or research environments.

### 1.3 Exceptions
Any exception to this Policy requires written approval from the Chief Information Security Officer and shall be reviewed no less than annually.

## 2. Governance

### 2.1 Accountability
The Chief Information Security Officer is accountable for this Policy and shall report on its effectiveness to the Board Audit Committee not less than twice per calendar year.

### 2.2 Risk assessment
Kestrel shall conduct a documented security risk assessment of all production systems at least annually, and following any substantial modification to a system that processes protected health information.

### 2.3 Policy review
This Policy and all subordinate policies shall be reviewed at least every twenty-four (24) months.

## 3. Technical Safeguards

### 3.1 Encryption in transit
All data transmitted outside a Kestrel-controlled network shall be encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.

### 3.2 Encryption at rest
All storage containing protected health information shall be encrypted at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent approved algorithm.

### 3.3 Vulnerability management
Critical vulnerabilities in production systems shall be remediated within fifteen (15) calendar days of identification; high severity within thirty (30) days.

### 3.4 Secure development
All code changes to systems processing protected health information shall undergo peer review before deployment to production.

## 4. Workforce Requirements

### 4.1 Security training
All workforce members shall complete security and privacy awareness training within thirty (30) days of hire and annually thereafter.

### 4.2 Sanctions
Failure to comply with this Policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract.
