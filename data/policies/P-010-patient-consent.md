## 1. Notice of Privacy Practices

### 1.1 Provision of notice
A Notice of Privacy Practices shall be provided to each individual no later than the date of first service delivery.

### 1.2 Acknowledgement
A good faith effort shall be made to obtain written acknowledgement of receipt, and any failure to obtain it shall be documented with the reason.

## 2. Authorization

### 2.1 When required
A valid written authorization is required for any use or disclosure of PHI not otherwise permitted by law.

### 2.2 Required elements
An authorization shall describe the information to be disclosed, the recipient, the purpose, an expiry date or event, and the individual's right to revoke.

### 2.3 Revocation
An individual may revoke an authorization in writing at any time, except to the extent Kestrel has already acted in reliance on it.

### 2.4 Compound authorizations
An authorization shall not be combined with another document to create a compound authorization, except as expressly permitted by law.

## 3. Special Categories

### 3.1 Psychotherapy notes
Use or disclosure of psychotherapy notes requires a separate authorization specific to those notes.

### 3.2 Substance use disorder records
Records subject to federal substance use disorder confidentiality rules shall be segregated and released only under the consent requirements applicable to those records.

### 3.3 Minors
Where a minor may lawfully consent to a service, the minor's authorization governs disclosure of records relating to that service.

## 4. Records

### 4.1 Retention of authorizations
Signed authorizations and revocations shall be retained in accordance with the Data Retention and Disposal Policy.
